Status: **requires explicit research approval before adoption or confirmation**. This is the Phase-04R Branch-B2 recommendation derived from the bounded cause review, not a replacement of `phase04/GATE_A_PARITY_RULE.md` and not a passing Gate A.

1. Define a B1, checkpoint/seed/prompt/precision/policy-bound depth trace as a **canonical frozen workload**. It is not the unique live depth that every legal microbatch composition must reproduce. Keep the original BF16 B1-versus-B8 FAILs visible.
2. Call a depth-sorted optimum computed on that fixed trace a **frozen-work packing Oracle**, not a live-adaptive Oracle. Its RIC compares grouping of one prescribed work matrix only. A live counterfactual Oracle, if studied, must execute each counterfactual grouping with its actual gate decisions and report the induced work.
3. Any resumed predictor may use causal pre-recurrence features only. Its target is depth propensity under a declared canonical batching/teacher-forcing policy; explicitly measure transfer to other B and compositions. Request-disjoint training/validation/confirmation rules remain mandatory.
4. Physical FIFO/Predictor/Oracle comparisons must report actual live depth/work/RIC, output-token differences, synchronized decode and full-workload wall time, and all feature/order overhead. Do not compare unequal live work as if it were an equal-work packing speedup. Retain a separate frozen-replay systems benchmark if useful.
5. A prospective Branch-B2 implementation release gate would require exact tokenized inputs, h0 identities, logical request↔KV-row and valid-position bookkeeping, no missing/duplicated rows, finite states/scores, independently checked exit comparator/cap behavior, and explicit duplicate/permutation controls. Depth and next-token flips versus B1 would be retained as **measured outcomes**, not silently corrected. The precise acceptance checks and fresh confirmation cohort must be committed *after approval and before execution*; they are not defined or applied by this draft.

The current Phase-4 predictor/speed hard block stays in force.
