## Pre-registered decision rule (tree.json)
For each (model, B, arm) vs AR on the SAME cohorts: paired cohort bootstrap speedup CI and paired
prompt bootstrap accuracy-delta CI (2000 resamples).
- `supported_heldout_quality_matched`: speedup CI low > 1.03 AND accuracy-delta CI low > −0.03.
- `exact` label only if sequence-exact rate is 1.0 and per-depth KV parity holds (no candidate
  arm qualifies: candidate KV carry is approximate by G0_KV/V2 evidence).
- otherwise `inconclusive_or_no_speedup` / `contradicted_quality`; CI crossing a margin is
  reported as inconclusive, not pass.
